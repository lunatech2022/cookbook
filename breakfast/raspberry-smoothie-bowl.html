Raspberry Smoothie Bowl

⏱️ 5 min 📂 Breakfast 🍽️ 2 servings

🏷️ Tags

📦 Batch Friendly 🌅 Breakfast 🧒 Kid Friendly 🧊 No Cook ⚡ Quick ⚡ Under 15 min 🌿 Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 2 cups frozen raspberries
  • 2 bananas
  • 1/2 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds (optional)
  • 1/2 cup lowfat milk
  • Granola
  • Toasted coconut flakes

Instructions

  1. **Blend:** In a blender, puree the raspberries, bananas, yogurt, chia seeds (if using), and milk until smooth.
  2. **Serve:** Divide between bowls and top with granola and toasted coconut, if desired.

🧒 Kid Adaptation

  • Let kids pick their own toppings (granola, coconut, fresh fruit, mini chocolate chips)
  • Use fun bowls or mason jars for serving
  • Add a drizzle of honey for extra sweetness
  • Freeze extra into popsicle molds for a grab-and-go treat

📦 Batch Cooking

  • Blend a big batch and freeze in individual portions
  • Thaw 10-15 min before serving, or blend again with a splash of milk
  • Prep toppings in small containers for quick assembly
