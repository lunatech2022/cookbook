Baked Breakfast Puddings

~370

🏷️ Tags

📦 Batch Friendly 🌅 Breakfast 🥩 High Protein 🧒 Kid Friendly ⏱️ 16-30 min 🌿 Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 1 heaped tbsp Greek yoghurt (40g)
  • 3 tbsp milk (45ml)
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • ¼ cup self-raising flour (40g)
  • 2 tbsp rolled oats (20g)
  • Big squeeze honey or maple syrup
  • A few frozen or fresh raspberries & blueberries

Instructions

  1. **Preheat:** Oven to **180°C fan**. Grease 1 small oven-proof dish (or 4 if multiplying).
  2. **Mix:** In the dish, combine egg, yoghurt, milk, vanilla, flour, oats, and honey. Mix well with a fork until smooth.
  3. **Top:** Sprinkle a few berries on top — don't add too many or the puddings will be soggy.
  4. **Clean edges:** Wipe any raw flour mix from the sides of the dish so it bakes evenly.
  5. **Bake:** 20 minutes. Test with a skewer — when it comes out clean, they're cooked.
  6. **Serve:** Leave to cool for 10 minutes. Top with a spoon of yoghurt and a drizzle of honey. Enjoy hot or cold.

🧒 Kid Adaptation

  • Let kids choose their own berry toppings
  • Serve with extra yoghurt on the side for dipping
  • Cut into squares if baked in a larger tin — easy handheld pieces
  • Add a few dark chocolate chips on top for a treat

📦 Batch Cooking

  • Multiply by 4 and bake in an **8-inch square tin**, or use 4 small ramekins
  • Store covered in the fridge for up to **3 days**
  • Reheat: 30-45 seconds in the microwave, or enjoy cold straight from the fridge
  • Do not freeze — texture becomes rubbery
