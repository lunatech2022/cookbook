No-Bake Energy Balls

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups whole rolled oats (old-fashioned)
  • 1 cup creamy natural peanut butter
  • ¼ cup ground flaxseed
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ¼ cup mini chocolate chips
  • 2 tbsp chopped peanuts
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened shredded coconut
  • Pinch sea salt (optional, if peanut butter is unsalted)

Instructions

  1. **Mix:** In a large bowl, combine oats, peanut butter, flaxseed, honey, vanilla, chocolate chips, peanuts, and coconut. If peanut butter is unsalted, add a pinch of salt. Mix until well combined — the mixture will be thick.
  2. **Roll:** Use a 2-tablespoon cookie scoop to portion the mixture, then roll into balls with your hands.
  3. **Chill:** Place on a plate or baking sheet and chill in the fridge for 1-2 hours until firm.
  4. **Store:** Keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to a week.

🧒 Kid Adaptation

  • Great recipe to make with kids — let them roll the balls
  • Substitute sunflower seed butter for nut-free schools
  • Use dairy-free chocolate chips to make vegan
  • Add a pinch of cinnamon or ground ginger for extra flavor

📦 Batch Cooking

  • Double the batch and freeze half
  • Freeze in an airtight container for up to 3 months
  • Thaw in minutes at room temperature
  • Prep "energy ball jars" — portion into small containers for grab-and-go
