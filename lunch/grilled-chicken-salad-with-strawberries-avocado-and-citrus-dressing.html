Grilled Chicken Salad with Strawberries, Avocado and Citrus Dressing

⏱️ 25 min 📂 Lunch 🍽️ 2 servings
~671
Calories
37g
Protein
11g

36g

🏷️ Tags

🌾 High Fiber 🥩 High Protein 🥗 Lunch ⏱️ 16-30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 Navel Oranges
  • 2 media lemons
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh basil chopped Optional substitute: dried basil
  • 1 ⁄ 4 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper Freshly ground
  • 1 ⁄ 4 cup extra-virgin olive oil Optional substitute (pending allergies): avocado, canola, grapeseed oil.
  • 1 boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 1 tablespoon Olive oil spray Optional substitute (pending allergies): avocado, canola, grapeseed oil.
  • 2 tablespoons raw slivered almonds ( Pre Packaged Bulk)
  • 1 ⁄ 3 teaspoon Kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper Freshly ground
  • 1 head butter lettuce chopped
  • 2 ounces Hass avocado sliced ( PRODUCE)
  • 1 cup strawberries sliced Optional substitute (pending allergies): blueberries, raspberries, blackberries
  • 1 ⁄ 4 cup red onion sliced

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl or measuring cup, whisk together the dressing ingredients together.
  2. Place chicken in a shallow bowl or Ziploc bag, add ½ the dressing and marinate chicken for 30 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, spray a medium skillet lightly with olive oil. Place skillet over medium low heat, add the almonds, a pinch of salt and a generous amount of fresh pepper. Toast almonds until they just start to brown and become fragrant, 4-5 minutes.
  4. Remove from heat, transfer almonds to a plate or your cutting board to cool.
  5. Spray the skillet with olive oil, remove chicken from marinade (discarding excess) and cook chicken over medium high heat for 3 minutes.
  6. Flip and cook an additional 2 minutes, or until chicken is browned and cooked through. Set aside on a cutting board to rest while you assemble the salad.
  7. In 2 bowls or meal prep containers, divide the lettuce then top each with ½ the avocado, strawberries, and red onion.
  8. Thinly slice the chicken breast and add to the salad.
  9. To eat immediately:
  10. Drizzle each with ½ the reserved dressing and top with ½ the slivered almonds.
  11. For meal prep:
  12. Store reserved dressing and almonds separately and top with both when ready to eat.
