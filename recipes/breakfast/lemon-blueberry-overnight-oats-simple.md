## Nutrition
- Calories: ~385
- Protein: 31g
- Fiber: 10g
- Carbs: 52g

## Ingredients
- 1 cup plain 0% Greek yogurt
- ¼ cup unsweetened almond milk
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- Zest of ½ lemon
- Pinch cinnamon
- ½ cup rolled oats
- ¾ tbsp chia seeds
- ½ cup blueberries

## Instructions
1. Stir together yogurt, almond milk, vanilla, lemon zest, and cinnamon.
2. Mix in oats and chia seeds.
3. Fold in blueberries.
4. Refrigerate overnight. Top with extra berries before serving if desired.

## Kid Adaptation
- Use less lemon zest for milder flavor
- Let kids add their own toppings
- Use a clear jar so they can see the layers
- Call it "Blueberry Pudding Breakfast"

## Batch Cooking
- Make 3-4 jars at once for the week
- Keeps in fridge up to 4 days
- Do not freeze
