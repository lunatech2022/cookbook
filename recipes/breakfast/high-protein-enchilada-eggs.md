## Nutrition
- Calories: ~328
- Protein: 33g
- Fiber: 6g
- Carbs: 12g

## Ingredients
- 1 cup egg whites
- 1 ⁄ 8 teaspoon adobo seasoning
- 1 ⁄ 4 cup homemade enchilada sauce
- 2 tablespoons cheddar cheese Optional substitute (pending allergies): American, Swiss, plant-based cheese alternative, etc..
- 1 ⁄ 4 cup scallion (chopped)
- 1 ⁄ 2 avocado

## Instructions
1. Beat egg whites with adobo seasoning. If you use all egg whites, you can add a little sazon to the egg for color, optional.
2. Spray a small skillet with oil over medium high heat.
3. When hot add the eggs and cook on medium heat until just set, stirring, about 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Top with enchilada sauce, cheese and cover the skillet.
5. Cook low until the cheese melts, 1 to 2 minutes.
6. Remove from heat and top with avocado and scallions.
