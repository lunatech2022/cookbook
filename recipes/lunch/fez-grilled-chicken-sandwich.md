## Nutrition
- Calories: ~487
- Protein: 25g
- Fiber: 8g
- Carbs: 52g

## Ingredients
- 3 ounces chicken breast flattened to 1/2 inch thick
- 1 ⁄ 2 teaspoon pepper
- 1 teaspoon olive oil Optional substitute (pending allergies): avocado, canola, grapeseed oil.
- 1 tablespoon red onion thinly sliced
- 1 ⁄ 2 lemon juiced
- 3 olives pitted and diced
- 1 ⁄ 4 cup parsley chopped Optional substitute: dried parsley
- 1 1 ⁄ 4 cups cucumber sliced
- 1 Gluten Free Hamburger Buns
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon mayonnaise
- 1 ⁄ 4 teaspoon cumin
- 1 ⁄ 4 teaspoon chili powder

## Instructions
1. Season chicken with pepper. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat and cook chicken for 3 minutes on each side or until cooked through.
2. Mix together red onion, lemon juice, olives, cucumber, and parsley.
3. Spread tomato paste on one slice of bread and mayonnaise on the other.
4. Place chicken between bun and sprinkle with cumin and chili powder. Top with olive salad.
5. Serve remaining salad on the side.
