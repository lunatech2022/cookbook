## Nutrition
- Calories: ~237
- Protein: 14g
- Fiber: 4g
- Carbs: 28g

## Ingredients
- 1 zucchini
- 1 egg Optional substitute (pending allergies): egg whites, egg beaters, plant-based egg alternative
- 1 ⁄ 4 cup water
- 1 tablespoon Parmesan cheese grated Optional substitute (pending allergies): pecorino, plant-based cheese alternative, etc..
- 1 ⁄ 4 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1 cooking spray

## Instructions
1. Lightly grease a baking sheet with cooking spray.
2. Cut zucchini into sticks about 3 inches long.
3. Microwave zucchini sticks for 3 minutes. Pat dry with a paper towel.
4. Add egg and water to a small bowl and whisk together.
5. In a separate bowl, combine cheese, breadcrumbs, and Italian seasoning.
6. Dip zucchini sticks into egg mixture and then coat in breadcrumbs.
7. Arrange zucchini sticks in rows on prepared baking sheet. Broil for 12-15 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden brown and crispy.
