---
title: "Mashed Cauliflower"
total_time: "20 min"
servings: "4"
dietary: "Vegetarian, Nut-Free"
---

## Nutrition
- Calories: ~75
- Protein: 4g
- Fiber: 3g
- Carbs: 9g

## Ingredients
- 1 head medium (5-6" dia.) cauliflower Optional substitute (pending allergies): broccoli, brussels sprouts, asparagus If out of season in your area, use frozen
- 4 cloves garlic crushed
- 1 ⁄ 3 cup 1% buttermilk
- 1 pinch salt and pepper ( Jfc)
- 1 tablespoon salted butter

## Instructions
1. Steam or boil cauliflower and garlic until soft.
2. Drain the cauliflower, then add buttermilk, butter, salt, pepper and purée with an immersion blender. (If you don't own a hand blender, you should consider getting one. It is one of my most used gadgets in my kitchen, you'll wonder how you ever lived without it!) A regular blender would work fine as well.
