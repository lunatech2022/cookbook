## Nutrition
- Calories: ~206
- Protein: 3g
- Fiber: 5g
- Carbs: 33g

## Ingredients
- 1 sweet potato Optional substitute (pending allergies): butternut squash, pumpkin, white potato or other root vegetable
- 3 ⁄ 4 teaspoon paprika
- 1 dash salt
- 1 ⁄ 2 tablespoon olive oil Optional substitute (pending allergies): avocado, canola, grapeseed oil.
- 1 cooking spray

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
2. Scrub and rinse sweet potato and pat dry. Cut into thick French fry strips, about 1/2-inch wide.
3. In a large bowl, mix paprika and salt. Add oil and blend with a fork until there are no lumps.
4. Add sweet potatoes to the bowl and toss to coat all sides.
5. Grease a baking sheet with cooking spray. Arrange sweet potatoes in a single layer on the baking sheet.
6. Bake 15 minutes, flip fries over, and bake another 10-15 minutes until tender.
