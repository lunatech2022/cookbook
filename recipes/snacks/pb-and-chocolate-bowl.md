## Nutrition
- Calories: ~217
- Protein: 22g
- Fiber: 5g
- Carbs: 25g

## Ingredients
- Meal Ingredients
- 170 grams plain 0% Greek yogurt
- 1 ⁄ 2 tablespoon Powdered Peanut Butter ( PB2)
- 5 grams unsweetened cocoa powder
- 5 grams chia seeds
- 5 grams mini chocolate chips
- 40 grams banana sliced
- 1 ⁄ 4 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions
1. Instructions
2. Mix the powdered peanut butter, cocoa powder, and vanilla into the Greek yogurt until smooth and creamy. Top with the banana, chia seeds, and mini chocolate chips.
