## Nutrition
- Calories: ~141
- Protein: 2g
- Fiber: 5g
- Carbs: 21g

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons salted butter
- 2 pounds carrots (peeled and sliced into 1-0.5 inch pieces, cut on the diagonal) If out of season in your area, use frozen or canned, no salt added in cooked recipes
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil Optional substitute (pending allergies): avocado, canola, grapeseed oil.
- 1 ⁄ 8 teaspoon kosher salt and black pepper
- 2 teaspoons fresh thyme
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley (finely chopped) Optional substitute: dried parsley
- 1 lemon Zest

## Instructions
1. Make sure the carrot pieces are of even thickness so they cook evenly.
2. Melt the butter in a large deep skillet with a fitted lid or medium pot over medium heat. Add the carrots, olive oil, salt, pepper, and thyme and toss to coat.
3. Cover and cook over medium to medium-low heat for 18 to 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the carrots are tender when pierced with a fork.
4. Stir in the honey and cook uncovered for 2 to 3 minutes, tossing until the carrots are lightly glazed.
5. Remove from heat and stir in the parsley and lemon zest. Serve warm.
