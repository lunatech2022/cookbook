## Nutrition
- Calories: ~261
- Protein: 21g
- Fiber: 6g
- Carbs: 31g

## Ingredients
- Meal Ingredients
- 170 grams plain 0% Greek yogurt
- 1 ⁄ 4 teaspoon Vanilla Extract optional
- 80 grams strawberries diced Optional substitute (pending allergies): blueberries, raspberries, blackberries
- 8 grams chia seeds
- 1 cracker graham crackers crushed Contains foods avoided in your taste profile
- 5 grams white chocolate chips

## Instructions
1. Instructions
2. Stir vanilla into the yogurt. Top with strawberries.
3. Sprinkle over chia seeds, graham crackers, and white chocolate chips.
