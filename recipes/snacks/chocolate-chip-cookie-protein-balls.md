## Nutrition
- Calories: ~80
- Protein: 4g
- Fiber: 1g
- Carbs: 12g

## Ingredients
- 1 cup rolled oats
- ½ cup powdered peanut butter (PB2)
- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp honey
- ¼ cup mini chocolate chips
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- Water (as needed, 1-2 tbsp)

## Instructions
1. **Mix:** In a large bowl, combine oats, powdered peanut butter, Greek yogurt, honey, chocolate chips, and vanilla. Mix until well combined. If mixture is crumbly, add 1-2 tbsp water or almond milk to help with binding.
2. **Chill:** Refrigerate mixture for 20 minutes to firm up.
3. **Roll:** Use your hands to roll into 12 balls.
4. **Store:** Keep in an airtight container in the fridge.

## Kid Adaptation
- Let kids help roll the balls — it's messy fun
- Use dairy-free chocolate chips and coconut yogurt for dairy-free
- Add a pinch of cinnamon for cookie flavor
- Roll in crushed cereal or sprinkles for extra kid appeal

## Batch Cooking
- Double the batch and freeze half
- Freeze in a single layer on a baking sheet, then transfer to a bag
- Thaw 5-10 minutes at room temperature
- Good for lunchbox additions or post-workout snacks
