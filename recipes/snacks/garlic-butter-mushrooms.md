---
title: "Garlic Butter Mushrooms"
total_time: "15 min"
servings: "4"
dietary: "Vegetarian, Nut-Free, Quick"
---

## Nutrition
- Calories: ~119
- Protein: 4g
- Fiber: 1g
- Carbs: 7g

## Ingredients
- 1 pound Cremini or Baby Bella mushrooms
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil Optional substitute (pending allergies): avocado, canola, grapeseed oil.
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper Freshly ground
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 4 large cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 bunch fresh Italian parsley Chopped Optional substitute: dried parsley

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, toss mushrooms with olive oil, salt and pepper to taste.
2. Add butter to a large skillet over medium heat.
3. When butter has melted and starts to foam, add the garlic.
4. Sauté garlic for 30 seconds then add the mushrooms. Toss to evenly coat.
5. Cook mushrooms for 10-12 minutes, stirring every 2 minutes to allow the edges to evenly caramelize.
6. Garnish with parsley and serve.
