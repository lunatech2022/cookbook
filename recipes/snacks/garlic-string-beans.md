## Nutrition
- Calories: ~102
- Protein: 2g
- Fiber: 3g
- Carbs: 9g

## Ingredients
- 1 pound fresh string beans (washed, ends trimmed) If out of season in your area, use frozen or canned, no salt added
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil Optional substitute (pending allergies): avocado, canola, grapeseed oil.
- 4 cloves garlic (sliced thin)
- 1 ⁄ 3 teaspoon salt and fresh pepper

## Instructions
1. Bring a large saucepan filled with 1 inch of water to a boil.
2. Lower a steamer basket filled with the green beans into it, tightly cover the pan, and steam for 4-5 minutes (don't overcook), until the beans are tender crisp. Drain.
3. In a saute pan heat olive oil. Add garlic and cook until golden.
4. Add string beans, salt and fresh pepper to taste and toss well.
