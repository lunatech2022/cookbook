## Nutrition
- Calories: ~436
- Protein: 38g
- Fiber: 7g
- Carbs: 51g

## Ingredients
- 1 pound boneless skinless chicken breasts (diced into 0.5 inch cubes)
- 1 teaspoon olive or canola oil Optional substitute (pending allergies): avocado, canola, grapeseed oil.
- 1 ⁄ 2 cup red onion diced
- 1 ⁄ 2 teaspoon paprika
- 1 ⁄ 2 teaspoon cumin
- 1 ⁄ 4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 cup canned low sodium black beans
- 9 1 tablespoons Buffalo Sauce
- 3 cups, cooked Brown Rice Cooked cooked
- 1 ⁄ 2 cup Shredded Part-Skim Mozzarella Cheese ( Kroger)
- 1 ⁄ 4 cup scallions chopped

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in a medium pot over medium-low heat. Add onions and cook until they have caramelized slightly, about 5 minutes.
2. Add black beans, paprika, cumin and salt; stir to combine and heat through, 3 to 4 minutes.
3. While that cooks, heat a large skillet over high heat, when hot spray with oil and add the chicken.
4. Cook until browned and cooked through in the center, about 5 minutes, flipping so it cooks on each side.
5. Set aside in a medium bowl and toss with the hot sauce.
6. To serve
7. Pour 3/4 cup rice in each bowl, top each with 1/4 cup beans and cheese, divide the chicken over the bowls.
8. If eating right away, I like to melt the cheese by putting it in the microwave 30 seconds. Top with scallions.
9. For meal prep
10. Refrigerate up to 4 days. To reheat, microwave about 3 minutes or until heated through.
