## Nutrition
- Calories: ~596
- Protein: 24g
- Fiber: 16g
- Carbs: 88g

## Ingredients
- 2 cups de-stemmed and, kale chopped ( Simple Truth Organic)
- 2 teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil Optional substitute (pending allergies): avocado, canola, grapeseed oil.
- 4 media sweet potatoes (peeled and quartered lengthwise (28 oz peeled)) Optional substitute (pending allergies): butternut squash, pumpkin, white potato or other root vegetable
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil Optional substitute (pending allergies): avocado, canola, grapeseed oil.
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon chipotle powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 ⁄ 2 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1 ⁄ 2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 cup plain 0% Greek yogurt
- 1 chipotle pepper in adobo sauce
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 2 cloves garlic (peeled)
- 1 pound No Sodium Black Beans drained and rinsed ( Target Stores)
- 4 cups Cooked Quinoa cooked ( World Variety Produce)
- 4 ounces avocado pitted, peeled, and diced
- 1 cup Quick-Pickled Red Onions
- 1 cup fresh cilantro leaves Optional substitute: dried coriander
- 1 lime (sliced into wedges)

## Instructions
1. Prepare the kale:
2. Place the kale, olive oil, and salt in a bowl. Massage the kale for 1 minute. It will shrink in volume.
3. Let sit at room temperature while making the rest.
4. Prepare the sweet potatoes:
5. Preheat the oven to 400°F with a rack placed in the center of the oven.
6. While the oven preheats, place the peeled and quartered sweet potatoes in a large saucepan and cover with water.
7. Bring to a boil over high heat. Boil the sweet potatoes just until the edges give a bit to pressure from a fork, about 8 minutes. You don’t want to overcook them, or they could turn mushy.
8. Drain the sweet potatoes and pat dry with a paper towel then cut them into bite-sized pieces.
9. In a large bowl, toss the sweet potatoes with 2 tablespoons of the olive oil.
10. In a small bowl, mix together the salt, chili powder, chipotle powder, cumin, coriander, and garlic powder.
11. Sprinkle the spice blend onto the sweet potatoes and toss to evenly coat.
12. Scatter the sweet potatoes on a sheet pan and roast for 15 to 20 minutes, flipping once halfway through, until browned and fork-tender.
13. Make the creamy chipotle sauce:
14. Place the yogurt, chipotle pepper, lime juice, garlic cloves, and a pinch of salt in a blender or small food processor. Blend or process until smooth.
15. Season to taste with salt. Set aside until ready to assemble the bowls, or store in the refrigerator.
16. Assemble the bowls:
17. Fill serving bowls with the kale, roasted sweet potatoes, black beans, quinoa, and avocado.
18. Garnish with the pickled red onions, creamy chipotle sauce, and cilantro. Serve with lime wedges.
