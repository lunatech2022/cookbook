## Ingredients
- For the Stir Fry
- 1 ⁄ 2 cup brown rice Optional substitute (pending allergies): other rice variety, quinoa, farro, millet, ect..
- 2 teaspoons olive oil, divided Optional substitute (pending allergies): avocado, canola, grapeseed oil.
- 1 ⁄ 2 pound chicken breast cut into chunks
- 1 ⁄ 2 bunch asparagus chopped Optional substitute (pending allergies): broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts If out of season in your area, use frozen
- 1 ⁄ 2 red bell pepper sliced
- 1 ⁄ 2 yellow bell pepper sliced
- 1 dash salt
- 1 dash pepper

## Instructions
1. To freeze
2. In a medium sauté pan on medium high heat add half the olive oil. Add in chicken and season with salt/pepper. Cook for 5-7 minutes or until chicken cooked through and no longer pink in the middle. Remove chicken from pan and set aside.
3. Blanch veggies in boiling water for 30-90 seconds.
4. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Spread out veggies and chicken on the baking sheet and freeze for 1 hour.
5. Meanwhile, prepare rice according to package directions. Let cool, then transfer to freezer bags or containers.
6. Next make the sauce. In a medium bowl add lime juice, orange juice, soy sauce, garlic and ginger and whisk to combine. Pour into a freezer bag and remove as much air as possible before closing.
7. Place the par-frozen chicken and veggies in separate freezer bags, removing as much air as possible before closing.
8. Place the bags of chicken, veggies, sauce, and rice in a large freezer bag or container. Freeze up to 3 months.
9. Thaw overnight in the fridge.
10. When ready to serve, heat rice in the microwave.
11. Spray a skillet with cooking spray and heat over medium. Add chicken and cook until heated through, then add in veggies and sauce, stirring frequently until heated through.
