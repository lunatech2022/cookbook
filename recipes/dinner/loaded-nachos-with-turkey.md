## Nutrition
- Calories: ~340
- Protein: 27g
- Fiber: 3g
- Carbs: 34g

## Ingredients
- 4 ounces baked tortilla chips
- 8 ounces 99% lean ground turkey breast ( Jennie-O Turkey Store)
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 ⁄ 3 teaspoon kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 1 ⁄ 3 cups reduced fat Mexican cheese blend
- 2 cups pico de gallo
- 1 ⁄ 2 cup light sour cream Optional substitute (pending allergies): Plain low fat Greek yogurt
- 1 ⁄ 2 cup fresh cilantro chopped Optional substitute: dried coriander
- 1 jalapeño

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425F.
2. Place 1 oz (about 14 depending on the brand) chips on four oven-proof dishes or all 4ounces on one large baking sheet.
3. Heat a large nonstick sauté pan over high heat.
4. Add the ground turkey and season with cumin, garlic powder, salt and pepper and sauté until cooked through, breaking up meat into small pieces with a wooden spoon, about 5 minutes.
5. Spoon the turkey mixture over the chips and sprinkle the cheese on top.
6. Bake until cheese melts, about 6 minutes.
7. Remove the nachos from the oven, top with pico de gallo, sour cream, cilantro and jalepeños.
8. Serve immediately.
