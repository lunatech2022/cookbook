## Nutrition
- Calories: ~460
- Protein: 32g
- Fiber: 3g
- Carbs: 33g

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon peanut butter Optional substitute (pending allergies): almond butter, cashew butter, sunbutter, ect..
- 1 tablespoon Low Sodium Soy Sauce
- 1 teaspoon sriracha
- 100 grams chicken breast cut into chunks
- 1 ⁄ 8 teaspoon Ground Black Pepper
- 2 tablespoons jasmine rice dry
- 1 ⁄ 4 cup chicken broth
- 1 ⁄ 4 cup Light Coconut Milk
- 1 teaspoon red curry paste
- 1 ⁄ 4 cup Broccoli cut into florets Optional substitute (pending allergies): cauliflower, Brussels sprouts, asparagus If out of season in your area, use frozen
- 1 ⁄ 4 cup Red Bell Peppers chopped
- 2 tablespoons White Onions cut into chunks

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350F. Lightly spray a 2 cup glass bowl with cooking spray.
2. In a small bowl mix together the peanut butter, soy sauce and sriracha - set aside.
3. Season the chicken breast with salt and pepper (to taste)
4. To the glass bowl add the rice, broth, coconut milk and red curry paste - mix well ensuring the rice remains towards the bottom of the bowl.
5. Add in the remaining ingredients and mix well.
6. Bake for 45-50minuts or until cooked through.
