One-Pot Chicken Orzo

⏱️ 65 min 📂 Dinner 🍽️ 4 servings
~372
Calories

3g

🏷️ Tags

🍽️ Dinner 🥩 High Protein 🥜 Nut Free 🍳 30+ min

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken thighs
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt (divided)
  • 1 ⁄ 2 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil Optional substitute (pending allergies): avocado, canola, grapeseed oil.
  • 1 ⁄ 2 cup onion (diced)
  • 1 cup orzo uncooked
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 ⁄ 2 cup tomato paste
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons pecorino romano grated
  • 1 ⁄ 4 cup basil Optional substitute: dried basil

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. season chicken
  3. Season chicken thighs with ¾ tsp of salt & bring to room temperature; thoroughly dry chicken skin with a paper towel before step 2.
  4. skillet
  5. Heat oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-low heat, add chicken thighs skin side down. Render until golden and crisp, about 12-15 minutes. Remove chicken from pan.
  6. add onions
  7. In the same pan, add onion and remaining salt and saute until aromatic, 4-5 minutes.
  8. add orzo
  9. Add orzo and toast 4-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until most pieces are lightly golden and there is a nutty aroma.
  10. make sauce
  11. Add garlic and mix to combine. Add tomato paste and cook to caramelize, 2-3 minutes.
  12. add broth and bake
  13. Add chicken broth & bring to a boil. Nestle chicken thighs, flesh side down, into the orzo (make sure that the skin is NOT in the liquid) and transfer to the oven Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until orzo is cooked through.*
  14. serve
  15. Serve in cooking vessel and top with grated cheese and basil.
